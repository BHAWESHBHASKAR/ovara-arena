Why a guard, if the model refuses?

Models are good. Good isn't enough.

In our live runs, Nemotron ignored most tricks on its own. But an agent runs thousands of steps a day, and it only has to be fooled once. OVARA doesn't depend on the model being right.

  • 🔑The agent never sees real keys. OVARA adds them on the way out, only for the service they belong to.
  • 🧪Untrusted text taints the agent. After it reads an issue anyone can write, its next upload or push waits for a person.
  • 🔍Secrets are spotted leaving. Even base64, hex or reversed, and even inside a call to the model itself.
  • 🧾Every decision is signed. A tamper-evident chain of receipts shows exactly what happened.
